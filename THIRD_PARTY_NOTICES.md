### AI voice disclosure

Both match commentators are synthetic (AI-generated) voices. Each is a weighted blend of Kokoro's stock voices.
They are not recordings of, clones of, or imitations of any real person or broadcaster, and all commentary lines
are original text written for OpenFootball. Store listings should say that the game uses AI-generated commentary
